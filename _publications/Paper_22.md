Abstract: This unit activity describes a series of lessons designed to enhance STEM students’ technical communication abilities. The lessons culminate in two separate yet interconnected projects: (1) the development of a children’s book explaining a complex topic and (2) presentations of those books to members of the local community. Students learn the characteristics defining communication for problem solving and are allowed to apply that knowledge across multiple modalities, focusing primarily on the interplay between audience analysis and the simplification of scientific concepts.
